AFTERLIGHT 1.3.0
A Minesweeper expedition

Run Afterlight.exe. Keep Afterlight.pck next to the executable.

Click a tile to reveal it. Right-click to flag a charge. A number counts charges
in all eight neighbouring tiles. Click a number with enough flags to chord.
The first opening is safe. Pulse (Space or 1) always opens safe ground.

Each field has two hull segments. A strike costs one segment, half your field
cargo, all energy and your chain. Losing both segments ends the attempt and
loses its remaining cargo. Retry immediately on a fresh layout. Banked light,
cores and purchased upgrades stay safe. Shield absorbs the first strike.

Every field is one board. A clear banks cargo and pays research cores: one in
the first two regions, two in the next two, three in the final two, plus bonuses.
Open Grow to spend banked resources. Click a node to pin its details and purchase
button; hover highlights without changing your selection. Arrows select nodes,
Enter connects them. Drag and scroll to explore the tree.

Tools use energy; safe manual reveals recharge it. Drones make logical deductions
and wait when they need an opening. Pulse recharges for free. Survey volley
replaces Autonomous descent: every new field gets three extra safe pulses.

Esc pauses. L opens the optional symbol legend; hover status icons for details.
F1 opens the guide. F11 toggles fullscreen. F10 captures a local field report.
Music, effects, reduced motion and high-contrast clues are in Settings.
Arrow keys select tiles; Enter/Space reveals and F flags the selected tile.
Tab opens Grow. Scroll over the field to zoom; middle-drag or use the overview
to pan. After Focused pulse, select Pulse and aim at the desired part of the field.

Your expedition saves automatically, including mid-board damage and cargo.
Older saves retain their current field, banked resources and upgrades. Clearing
that field completes the site; completed old strata become a finished site.
Windows saves: %APPDATA%/Godot/app_userdata/Afterlight/
Reports remain local. The game does not connect to external services.

There are 96 campaign fields, fifty upgrades, twelve optional mastery trials,
and an ending followed by endless exploration. Trials unlock after each region.

All artwork and audio are procedural and repository-owned. Godot and library
licences are available from Credits > Engine & library licences.
